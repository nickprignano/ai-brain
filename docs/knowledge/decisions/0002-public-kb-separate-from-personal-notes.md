# 0002. A public AI research KB, kept separate from personal notes

## Context and Problem Statement

The maintainer already keeps a private personal notes vault, which holds a few AI-technical notes
next to everything else in life. The family format assumes a public, reviewed KB in a repo. Should
the AI research KB be that vault, or something new?

## Considered Options

- A new public repo for AI research, with the personal vault kept private and out of the family
- Make the personal vault public and move it to the family format
- Keep AI research in the private vault only

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "A new public repo for AI research", because the family's rules (public, PR-only,
no-leak) fit research findings but not personal notes, and keeping them apart protects both.

### Consequences

- Good, because the AI findings can be shared and linked by the siblings, and the personal vault
  keeps its privacy.
- Good, because the vault's AI-technical notes move here once, re-checked for leaks, with a pointer
  left behind.
- Bad, because there are now two places to write; the rule is that AI research goes here and
  personal knowledge goes in the vault.
- Neutral, because this KB never links into the private vault, while the vault can link here.
